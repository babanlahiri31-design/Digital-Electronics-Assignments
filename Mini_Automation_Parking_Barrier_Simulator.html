4. Transition Logic

Inputs: V vehicle detected, A authorization valid, C path clear, E emergency stop.

Outputs: UP motor raises barrier, DN motor lowers barrier, G green indicator, R red indicator.

SignalLogic / meaning
UPState = OPENING
DNState = CLOSING AND C = 1 AND E = 0
GState = OPENING OR OPEN
RNOT G OR E
Safety overrideE = 1 forces motor STOP
Reopen conditionState = CLOSING AND C = 0

This is a Moore-style FSM: outputs primarily depend on the current state, with emergency and safety inputs acting as overrides.

7. Design Constraints & Edge Cases

  • Emergency stop has priority over all normal transitions and stops the motor.
  • The barrier must not close while the path sensor reports an obstruction.
  • An invalid ticket/payment must not open the barrier.
  • When the vehicle leaves and the path is clear, the barrier closes.
  • Inputs are sampled at each FSM clock pulse; the browser model does not simulate electrical delay or physical motor inertia.
